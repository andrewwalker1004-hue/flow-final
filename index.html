Flow

Every Drop Makes a Difference.

Flow is a water-allocation puzzle game inspired by charity: water's mission. Distribute limited clean water across hospitals, homes, schools, and factories — balance every community's needs and learn why access to water changes everything.

How to Play

  1. 1
    Limited supply. Each town has a limited amount of clean water available.
  2. 2
    Every building needs water. Hospitals, homes, schools, and factories each have a required demand.
  3. 3
    Allocate it. Use the sliders or the +/- buttons to send water to each building.
  4. 4
    Satisfy as many as you can. A building is "fully satisfied" only when it gets at least its full demand.
  5. 5
    Submit. Submit your distribution to calculate your efficiency score.
  6. 6
    Earn XP & unlock more. Earn XP based on your result and unlock new puzzles and towns.

About charity: water

charity: water is a real nonprofit organization working to bring clean and safe drinking water to people around the world. Access to clean water affects health, education, and economic opportunity for entire communities.

Flow is an independent, unofficial educational prototype created to raise awareness of water-access issues. It is not produced, endorsed, or verified by charity: water, and gameplay does not generate real donations or fund real projects.
